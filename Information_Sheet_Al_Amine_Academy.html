Our Programs

Elementary Program

"Yarr ak Xam-Xam" — Good character and knowledge

A quality academic program based on excellence and good behavior. The teaching is structured around the following subjects:

French English Quran Islamic Education Sports
To build self-confidence, we offer Self-Defense classes outside of regular school hours, allowing each child to ensure their safety on a daily basis.
Tahfiz Program

Give your child the opportunity to learn and memorize the Noble Quran in a caring and stimulating environment! The Tahfiz program is designed for children aged 5 to 10 and offers quality educational guidance, combining:

  • Complete memorization of the Quran
  • Learning the rules of Tajweed for correct and respectful recitation
  • Islamic education adapted to the students' age
  • Fun and educational activities promoting personal development
NB: Upon completion of Quran memorization, students have the opportunity to follow a three-year training program in preparation for the CFEE (Certificate of Elementary Studies).
